PRIVACY POLICY Last Updated: May 2026 Where is my Vehicle by Craftman Studios respects your privacy. This app is designed to help users save and find their parked vehicle quickly and securely. INFORMATION WE COLLECT The app may access: • Device location (to save and find parking spots) • Camera permission (for parking photos if enabled) • Local device storage We do not collect: • Name • Email address • Contacts • Messages HOW YOUR DATA IS STORED All parking information is stored locally on your device using secure local storage. CAMERA PERMISSION Camera access is optional and only used for parking reference photos.
