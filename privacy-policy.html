Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Where is my Vehicle by Craftman Studios respects your privacy. This app is designed to help users save and find their parked vehicle quickly and securely.

Information We Collect

The app may access:

  • Device location (to save and find parking spots)
  • Camera permission (for parking photos if enabled)
  • Local device storage

We do not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Contacts
  • Messages

How Your Data Is Stored

All parking information is stored locally on your device using secure local storage. Craftman Studios does not store your parking locations on external servers.

Location Permission

Location access is required for:

  • Saving parking spots
  • Navigation back to your vehicle

The app only uses location while using these features.

Camera Permission

Camera access is optional and only used for parking reference photos.

Subscription & Payments

Vehicle Pro is an optional premium subscription that unlocks:

  • Dark Mode
  • Future premium features
  • Ad-free experience

Payments are securely handled by Google Play Billing.

Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Maps services

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data and app security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of the app.
